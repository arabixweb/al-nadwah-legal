Founded in 1998

Founded in 1998 by Sheikh Abdullah Al-Nadwi, our firm began as a small practice in Riyadh's historic district. Over 25 years, we have grown into one of Saudi Arabia's most respected legal consultancies, serving over 3,000 clients across the Kingdom.

Our deep understanding of both Sharia law and modern legal frameworks allows us to serve our clients with unmatched precision. We combine traditional Saudi legal principles with international best practices.
